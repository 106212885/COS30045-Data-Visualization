Frequently Asked Questions

Heating and cooling systems typically account for around 40% of Australian household energy usage, followed by water heaters, refrigerators, and entertainment devices like televisions.

Multiply the wattage of your appliance by the average daily usage hours, divide by 1000 to convert to kilowatt-hours (kWh), and multiply by your retail tariff rate (e.g., $0.30 per kWh).

Modern screens are used for several hours daily. Choosing high energy-star-rated televisions helps reduce overall standby power draw and lowers household electricity bills.
