Telvisions Energy Usage

Comparing power consumption across common TV display technologies in Australia.

Energy Star Ratings

In Australia, all new televisions must display an Energy Rating Label. Choosing a TV with more starts can significally lower your electricity bill over the lifespan of the device.

Display Technology Comparison

LED and OLED screens generally consume less power compared to older Plasma displays. Screen size also plays a major role—larger screens draw more power regardless of panel technology.

Standby Power Draw

Many modern smart TVs enter a standby mode rather than fully turning off, continuously drawing small amounts of power to listen for remote commands or network updates.
