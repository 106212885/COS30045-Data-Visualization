SVG Shapes: Exercise 4.1

A house and garden scene built from SVG primitives, then customised to show how each shape and attribute can change.

Before and After

The same house layout is shown twice. The left drawing is the original picture built from basic SVG shapes. The right drawing is the customised version, produced by moving shapes, changing fill colours, adjusting stroke attributes, and adding new items such as the chimney and the path.

What Changed

The table below lists each shape that moved or was added, giving its position before and after customisation. Colour and stroke changes that did not involve a position change are described underneath the table.

The sky changed from skyblue to a light lavender, and the ground changed from forestgreen to a warmer green. The roof fill changed from chocolate to red and its stroke width increased from 2 to 3. The house body fill changed from saddlebrown to cream, and the door fill changed from peru to teal. The windows kept the exact same group structure in both versions: one outer <g> sets a shared stroke and fill for both windows, and each window sits in its own inner <g transform="translate(x,0)">, so the pair could be restyled without touching each shape individually.
